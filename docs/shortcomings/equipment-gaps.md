# Equipment integration gaps

## Physical attack statuses

Severed Chains resolves physical attack status application as one chance and one status mask. Irongoon therefore cannot roll equipment and addition statuses as independent simultaneous sources.

When addition status randomization is enabled, an equipped status mask always takes precedence. The addition status is suppressed even when the equipment status chance later fails. An addition status is considered only after the complete addition succeeds, and its campaign assignment is rolled at most once on that final successful attack.

Supporting multiple simultaneous status sources would require a broader Severed Chains status-result model and is outside addition randomization.
